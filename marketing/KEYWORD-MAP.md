# Keyword Map for foundery.space (2026)

> Target keyword clusters, intent, and the URL on foundery.space that should
> rank/cite for each. This is the source of truth for content planning.

---

## Intent Categories

We map every keyword to one of 4 intents:

- **I (Informational)** — "what is...", "how to..." — best for **pillar guides**
- **C (Commercial)** — "best X", "top Y" — best for **roundup pages**
- **T (Transactional)** — "apply to YC", "submit to Techstars" — best for **opportunity detail pages**
- **N (Navigational)** — "foundery.space", brand queries — best for **homepage**
