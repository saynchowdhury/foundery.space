# Content Cluster Plan for foundery.space (2026)

> Topic cluster architecture: 1 pillar page per major theme, with 5–10
> supporting articles each. Built around the AI Citation pattern: pillars
> cite the corpus, supporting articles cite the pillar, opportunities cite
> both.

---

## Why Topic Clusters

Topic clusters tell AI engines: "this site is the authoritative source on
this topic". The pillar + supporting pattern is the most-cited structure
across all AI engines (per recent GEO studies).

Each cluster has:
- **1 Pillar Page** (2,000–3,500 words, comprehensive)
- **5–10 Supporting Articles** (800–1,500 words, specific)
- **10+ Internal Links** in each direction
- **1 Direct Answer paragraph** at the top of every page
- **1 FAQ schema block** (4–8 Q-A) at the bottom of every page
- **1 Tier-2 citation** (Crunchbase, G2, Substack)
- **1 Tier-3 citation** (Wikipedia, .gov, .edu, major publisher)

## Cluster 8 — "[Category] for [Audience]" (Programmatic SEO)

For each combination of (category) × (audience), generate a programmatic page:

**Categories:** fellowship, grant, accelerator, incubator, competition, residency, research, developer-program
**Audiences:** first-time-founders, technical-founders, women, black-founders, latinx-founders, veterans, lgbtq+, students, international

**URLs:** `/{category}/{audience}` — e.g., `/grant/women`, `/accelerator/black`, `/fellowship/students`
**Content:** 500–800 words each, mostly templated with custom intro + FAQ
**Output:** ~70 programmatic pages

This is the **programmatic SEO play** — used by G2, NerdWallet, etc. Each page ranks for a specific long-tail query like "grants for women founders".

## Content Quality Bar

Every page must score 9/10 on the **AI Citation Quality Score**:

| Factor | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Direct Answer** | 40–120 word paragraph at top, before first H2 |
| **FAQ** | 4–8 Q-A at bottom, FAQPage schema |
| **H2 every 200–300 words** | Yes |
| **Numerical claims** | At least 1 every 150–200 words |
| **External citations** | ≥3 (with source URL + author + date) |
| **Internal links** | ≥10 to other foundery.space pages |
| **Schema** | Article + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage minimum |
| **dateModified** | Visible on page + in schema |
| **Word count** | 800+ for supporting, 2,000+ for pillars |
| **Tables** | At least 1 if data-heavy |
| **Images** | At least 1 per page (with alt text) |
| **Readability** | Flesch-Kincaid grade 8–10 |

## Internal Linking Map

Pillar pages link **out to all** their supporting articles.
Supporting articles link **up to their pillar** and **laterally to siblings**.
Opportunity pages link **up to their category** and **laterally to related opportunities**.
Blog posts link **up to relevant pillar + supporting**.

```
PILLAR ──┬── Supporting 1 ──┐
         ├── Supporting 2 ──┤
         ├── ...            ├──> All link to relevant opportunities
         └── Supporting N ──┘
              ↓
        All Opportunities (link back to pillar)
```
