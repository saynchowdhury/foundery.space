## 2026-06-22 - [Layout] Floating Action Button Stack
**Learning:** Secondary floating interactive elements (like 'Back to Top') should be offset vertically from the primary floating action (like 'Feedback').
**Action:** Position secondary floating buttons at `bottom-24 right-6` to maintain a clear 64px vertical gap from the primary `bottom-6 right-6` element, ensuring no visual collision and clear hit targets.
