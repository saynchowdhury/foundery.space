## 2026-06-08 - [Enhancing Interactivity & Accessibility]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (mobile menus, clear search buttons) are common accessibility gaps in this cyberpunk-themed UI. Adding toast feedback for state-tracking components (like `AppliedButton`) significantly improves the "technical" feel of the app.
**Action:** Always provide ARIA labels for icon buttons and use `sonner` for immediate feedback on persistent state changes.
